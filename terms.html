02 · Voice analysis and consent

Installing or signing up does not authorize voice analysis. You must separately enable analysis after reviewing the consent statement. The tools analyze acoustic characteristics of your own voice to estimate wellbeing signals. They do not transcribe the words you speak. Google Meet sampling uses your microphone only, while you are in a call and unmuted. Zoom analysis requires a connected Zoom account and explicit voice consent.

Do not use the service to analyze another person's voice without authorization. Meet and Zoom availability depends on platform permissions, account settings and marketplace approval.

03 · Audio and results

Voice samples are processed only to produce your results and operate the requested service. Raw audio is discarded after analysis. Your meeting audio and derived results are not used to train models. Derived results and consent records are kept while your account is active, subject to your deletion choices described in the Privacy Policy. This policy applies specifically to these meeting tools, including where another general Amplifier agreement describes different recording retention or training practices.
