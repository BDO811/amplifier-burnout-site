How these indices are calculated

The score method matches Serasona: readable sign bands are weighted into Stress, Wellness and Clarity. Normal = 100, Low = 68, Moderate = 38, Elevated = 8. Missing and inconclusive signs are excluded. Higher indices mean fewer flagged signs.

This is a provisional product index, not a validated clinical scale or a diagnosis. Only signs included in your plan and returned by analysis enter your score. A missing group stays unmeasured. Personal baselines and trends are shown only when the analysis service supplies enough history.
