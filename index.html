Why measurement fails today

Burnout has a clinical definition. Most companies never measure against it.

The World Health Organization classifies burnout in the ICD-11 as an occupational phenomenon, not a medical condition, resulting from chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed.7 It has a real, validated structure. The tools to measure it have existed for decades.

The problem has never been the science. It is the format. Every validated instrument depends on someone stopping, remembering how they have felt, and filling out a form, honestly, on a schedule nobody enforces. Executives are the population least likely to do that.

  • Maslach Burnout Inventory The field's gold standard. Scores exhaustion, cynicism, and reduced sense of accomplishment across 22 self-reported items.8
  • WHO ICD-11 Defines burnout as chronic, unmanaged workplace stress with three dimensions: exhaustion, mental distance from the job, and reduced efficacy.7
  • Copenhagen Burnout Inventory Separates personal, work-related, and client-related exhaustion into distinct, trackable subscales.9
  • Fatigue Severity Scale A short, widely used self-report scale for how fatigue interferes with motivation, physical function, and daily work.10

The science

Fatigue changes how you sound before you notice how you feel.

Speech is a motor task controlled by the same nervous system that governs stress and recovery. Decades of acoustic research link measurable, involuntary changes in voice to fatigue, cognitive load, and chronic stress.

Pitch & intonation

Pitch moves in both directions, and both are meaningful

Acute stress and cognitive load tend to raise pitch and intensity, while chronic fatigue flattens pitch range into a more monotone delivery. A systematic review of 39 studies found prosodic features showed the strongest link to stress, negative emotion, and cognitive load of any acoustic signal measured.11

Voice quality

Jitter & shimmer

Cycle-to-cycle instability in pitch and amplitude rises measurably after sleep deprivation, alongside a softer, lower-intensity voice, in controlled acoustic studies.12

Timing

Speech rate & pausing

Pause length and speech timing are central inputs to published voice-based fatigue detection models, with one study reporting up to 94% accuracy from acoustic signal alone.13

Energy

Vocal intensity

Reduced loudness and breath support track with low arousal and depleted energy, documented directly in sleep-deprivation research on vocal parameters.12

Spectral features

Harmonic structure

Harmonics-to-noise ratio and other spectral measures shift under physiological strain, features already used in voice-based stress and cognitive load models.11

Longitudinal trend

The person as their own baseline

No voice sounds "burned out" in isolation. The signal is a trend against a person's own established pattern, call after call.

Academic validation underway

Burnout and voice, direct research in progress

A preregistered University of Verona study is testing this exact pipeline: scoring pitch, jitter, shimmer, and spectral features extracted from short speech samples against the clinically validated Oldenburg Burnout Inventory, across seven time points over a 31-day period, in students and working employees.14

Zoom fatigue is real, and well documented

Stanford research named it. Amplifier is built to finally measure it.

Researchers at Stanford's Virtual Human Interaction Lab identified four specific drivers of video-call fatigue: excessive close-up eye contact, the cognitive load of exaggerating gestures for the camera, constant self-view, and reduced physical mobility.15 The same team then built and validated the Zoom Exhaustion & Fatigue Scale, a 15-item instrument tested on 2,724 people, to actually measure it.16

That research proved video calls carry a unique fatigue signature. Amplifier is the layer that reads it directly from the one channel every call already produces: audio.

Already a normal part of the call

Recording and transcription are no longer unusual on a Zoom or Google Meet call.

Call recording, AI note-taking, and transcript generation are now standard features on most executive calendars, Otter.ai alone has transcribed more than a billion meetings.17 Amplifier uses separated meeting audio to generate private wellness signals after the user explicitly consents.

Amplifier measures how something is said. Never what was said.
