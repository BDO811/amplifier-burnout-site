DocumentationAdding, using, and removing Amplifier.

Covers the Amplifier app for Zoom and the Amplifier for Google Meet Chrome extension

Adding the app

Zoom

  1. Go to burnout.amplifierhealth.com and select Add to Zoom, or install from the Zoom App Marketplace listing.
  2. Sign in to Zoom and authorize the requested permissions. Zoom returns you to Amplifier automatically.
  3. Create an Amplifier account with your email and a password, or sign in if you already have one, at burnout.amplifierhealth.com/account.
  4. In your account dashboard, read the consent notice and turn on voice analysis. Analysis stays off until you do this step.

Google Meet

  1. Install Amplifier for Google Meet from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the extension icon, sign in with your Amplifier account (or create one), and agree to the consent notice.
  3. Join a Google Meet call. Select Start in the extension popup once you are unmuted.

Using Amplifier

Once consent is on, Amplifier samples about 30 seconds of audio at the start of a call and 10 seconds every 60 seconds after that, your own voice only. In Zoom this runs automatically in meetings you host once you are an authorized, consenting participant; in Google Meet you start and stop it from the extension popup.

Your dashboard at burnout.amplifierhealth.com/account shows daily Stress and Fatigue readings, trend history, and plan usage. History depth follows your plan: Spark (free) keeps 7 days, Groove 90 days, Encore 270 days. Manage your plan, payment method, and invoices from the billing link in your account settings.

You can pause analysis at any time: use the Stop button in the Google Meet extension popup, or withdraw consent from your account dashboard to stop Zoom capture.

Removing the app

Zoom

  1. Sign in to the Zoom App Marketplace.
  2. Select Manage in the top right, then Added Apps.
  3. Find Amplifier and select Remove.

Removing the app stops all future voice analysis immediately. Your past Stress and Fatigue results stay in your Amplifier account; they are not deleted by removing the Zoom connection. To delete your account and its data entirely, email the address below.

Google Meet

Open Chrome's extensions page, find Amplifier for Google Meet, and select Remove. You can also select Sign out or Withdraw consent from the extension popup first if you want to stop analysis without uninstalling.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens after I select Start. Confirm you are signed in and have agreed to the consent notice; analysis stays off until consent is explicitly given.
  • Zoom shows no readings. RTMS capture only runs for the Zoom account that installed and authorized the app, and only once that user has turned on analysis in their dashboard.
  • Google Meet extension shows "Cannot read your Meet mute button." Reload the Meet tab; the extension needs to detect your mute state before it will capture audio.
  • I can't sign in. Use Forgot password? on the account page, or contact support below.
